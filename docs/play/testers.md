# Closed-test invite

Google requires a new developer account to run a closed test with at least 12 testers, opted in for 14 days in a row, before the app can go public on Play. This is the message to send testers. Replace `OPT-IN-LINK` with the link Play Console shows under *Testing → Closed testing → Testers → "How testers join your test"*.

Testers must use the Google account you added to the group, install from the Play link (not the GitHub APK), and keep the app installed and opted in for the full 14 days. If they uninstall, the day count stops.

---

Hi! I'm testing an app I made and need a few people to try it for two weeks. It's called **LinkGuard**: when you tap a link in WhatsApp or SMS, it checks the link first and warns you if it looks like a scam (fake bank pages, "update your KYC" apps, UPI payment tricks). Safe links open normally, and it never saves or sends the links you open.

What to do:
1. Open this link on your Android phone and tap **Become a tester**: OPT-IN-LINK
2. Install LinkGuard from the Play Store page it takes you to.
3. Open LinkGuard and tap **Set as default browser**. Pick Chrome (or your usual browser) as the browser links should open in.
4. Keep it installed for at least **14 days**. Use your phone as normal; you don't need to do anything else.

If you get a scam message, tap its link: you should see a warning, and you can tap **Report this link** to help others. You can always tap **Open anyway**.

Things I'd love to hear about:
- A safe link that showed a warning, or felt slow to open
- A scam link that opened with no warning (use **Report a link** in the app)
- Anything confusing, or text in your language that reads wrong (**Settings → Wrong translation? Tell us**)

Reply here, or email support@dwarpallabs.com. The app is free and open source: https://dwarpallabs.com

Thank you!
