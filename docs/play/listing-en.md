## Title
LinkGuard: Scam Link Checker

## Short description
Checks WhatsApp and SMS links for scams before they open. It never blocks.

## Full description
Got a link on WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS and not sure if it's safe? LinkGuard checks every link you tap, in a split second, before it opens.

Safe links open straight away in your usual browser and you see nothing. If a link looks like a scam, you get one clear warning in English or in any of India's 22 official languages, explaining why. LinkGuard never blocks: you can always choose "Open anyway".

WHAT LINKGUARD CATCHES
• Fake bank, wallet and delivery websites that copy well-known names, including look-alike letters, swapped numbers and small spelling changes
• App downloads (APK files) from outside the Play Store, a common trick in "update your KYC" messages
• UPI payment requests hidden behind links, showing who you would pay and how much before you approve anything
• Known harmful websites, from a list of about 1,900 collected from a leading international security database and updated every day
• Unusual web addresses, such as raw number addresses and throwaway domains
• Brand-new websites, which scammers use for a few days and then abandon
• Links reported as scams by several LinkGuard users in the same week
• Short links (bit.ly and others) are followed first, so the real destination is checked

LINKGUARD IS NOT A BROWSER
Android lists LinkGuard as your "browser app" only so it can see links first. It never shows web pages. Every link still opens in the browser you already use, such as Chrome, and your bookmarks, history, saved passwords and tabs stay exactly as they are.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• LinkGuard never keeps a history of the links you open and never sends them to us.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
• Most checks run on your phone. For unfamiliar websites, LinkGuard sends only a short, scrambled fingerprint of the website's name, so we cannot tell which site you visited.
• Reports are anonymous: your reports can't be linked together or traced back to you.

OPEN SOURCE
LinkGuard is free and open source (Apache-2.0). Anyone can check that it does what it says: github.com/dwarpallabs/linkguard

HOW TO START
1. Open LinkGuard and tap "Set as default browser".
2. Choose the browser your links should open in.
3. Apps like Telegram, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn often open links inside the app, where LinkGuard can't check them. Turn off their in-app browser in the app's settings, or choose "Open in browser".

Works on Android 8 and newer, including phones with 2 GB of memory.

YOUR LANGUAGE
LinkGuard follows your phone's language: English, Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, Dogri, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Konkani, Maithili, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Santali, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu.
